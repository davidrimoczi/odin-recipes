Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna, lasagna. How I love thee! It is possibly one of the most loved foods in the whole wide world, and understandably so. There is just something so sentimental about lasagna, so comforting.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Beef
  • Canned Tomato
  • Seasonings

Steps

  1. Create the sauce like a bolognese
  2. Layer it up
  3. Top it with lasagna sheets and pour remaining white sauce
  4. Pop it in the oven
